# 03-DECISIONS

Decision records: the "why" trail behind the META postures and the DESIGN docs. A record captures the context, the decision made, the alternatives rejected, and the consequences — written at the moment something significant settles: a research graduation, a design amendment, a how-we-build posture.

The records use the ADR (Architecture Decision Record) format and keep their historical numbering.

## Rules

- Records are **immutable once made** — superseded by a new record, never edited. (Fixing broken links or paths is allowed; changing meaning is not.)
- Files are numbered sequentially: `NNNN-short-name.md`. Use the next free number; never reuse one.
- A decision that changes design or process is recorded here before or alongside that change — see playbook [`00-META/process/02-graduation.md`](../00-META/process/02-graduation.md).
